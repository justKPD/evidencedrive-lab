WHAT THE MODEL DOES — AND DOES NOT — SAY

A small model.
Explicit boundaries.

  • Two abstract pressure channels with first-order response, shared or independent supply, explicit signal faults and recovery.
  • Both channels represent redundant capacity. Their pressure is not added to double the available braking.
  • Friction and nonnegative speed constrain the computed motion. Every injected event has a visible time and target.
  • Source clauses are authored examples, not Bosch requirements. No physical brake validation, HIL or safety certification is asserted.
  • Browser hosting computes in JavaScript. The local service can execute the independent Python implementation; the current engine is labeled.
